Location

Venue: Sporthal St. Eduardus Instituut
Address: Broeder Frederikstraat 3, 2170 Merksem

Public Transport: Tram 2 or 3A: Get off at stop Merksem Oudebareel. Use the website De Lijn to plan your itinerary.

Parking: Free car parking is available on-site at the venue.

Tournament Levels & Categories

All levels are gender-neutral (the levels indicated are those of men's doubles):

  • Doubles A
  • Doubles B+
  • Doubles B-
  • Doubles C+
  • Doubles C-

Rules & Format

  • Morning: Poule (Group) phase. Every team plays multiple matches to determine seeding.
  • Afternoon: Knock-out phase. All teams advance to the afternoon brackets, ensuring a full day of play for everyone.
  • Official Match Shuttle: All levels will play with Victor Champion No. 1 (feather shuttlecocks). Exception for the C- Level: Matches may be played with a Yonex Mavis 300 (Plastic shuttlecock), only if all players on the court unanimously agree before the match begins. If there is no total agreement, feather shuttles must be used.

Catering & Extra's

  • Lunch Break: Our famous buffet-style lunch is back! It will be provided for all players during the break between the poule phase and the knock-out phase.
  • Free Refreshments: Extra sweets, fruits, water, and coffee will be available throughout the day.
  • Venue Cafeteria: Here you can get something to take care of any other cravings or extra drinks you might want during the event.

Entry Fee & Registration Policy

  • Price: €50 per player.
  • What is included: Participation in the tournament, official tournament goodie bag, and access to the buffet lunch.
  • Withdrawal Policy: Refunds cannot be guaranteed for cancellations made after October 1st, 2026.
  • Successful Registration: Your registration is complete once payment is received. A spot in a division is reserved only when both team members have completed their registration and payment (we recommend registering at the same time as your partner).
