Timing & Schedule

  • Registration starts: July 21st, 2026 at 10:00 CEST. *(You will need your double partner's name and email. Each player registers separately; no blind dates).*
  • Tournament Day: Saturday, November 7, 2026

Tournament Day Schedule

  • 08:00: Doors open
  • 08:30: End of registration verification
  • 08:45: Tournament briefing / info session
  • 09:00: Start tournament (Strictly programmed, please be on time!)

Location

Venue: Sporthal St. Eduardus Instituut

Address: Broeder Frederikstraat 3, 2170 Merksem

Public Transport: Tram 2 or 3A: Get off at stop Merksem Oudebareel. Plan your itinerary via De Lijn.

Parking: Free car parking is available right on-site at the venue.

Tournament Levels & Categories

All levels are gender-neutral (categories correspond to standard men's doubles benchmarks):

  • Doubles A
  • Doubles B+
  • Doubles B-
  • Doubles C+
  • Doubles C-

Rules & Format

  • Morning Phase: Poule (Group) phase. Every team plays multiple matches to determine placement.
  • Afternoon Phase: Knock-out phase. All teams advance to afternoon brackets to guarantee a full day of playing.
  • Shuttlecocks: Official match shuttle is the Victor Champion No. 1 (feather). Exception for C- Level: Matches may use Yonex Mavis 300 (plastic) only if all players unanimously agree prior to the match.

Catering & Extras

  • Lunch Break: Enjoy our famous buffet-style lunch provided for all players between the group and knock-out stages.
  • Refreshments: Free sweets, fruits, water, and coffee available throughout the day.
  • Cafeteria: Venue cafeteria will be open for extra cravings or drinks.

Entry Fee & Registration Policy

  • Price: €50 per player.
  • Included: Tournament entry, official tournament goodie bag, and the full buffet lunch.
  • Withdrawal Policy: Refunds cannot be guaranteed for cancellations requested after October 1st, 2026.
  • Reservation Rule: A slot in a division is only secured when both partners have completed registration and payment.
