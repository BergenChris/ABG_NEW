ABG26 takes place from 6 November until 8 November 2026.

The official ABG26 website is http://www.antwerpbrilliantgames.be. Information on this site takes precedence over any other communication channel.

Registration and Payments

Registrations are made online and require a valid and unique email address. All registrations are individual, except for volleyball teams. Volleyball team captains can register their entire team.

Online registration is open from 21 July 2026 until 10 October 2026, unless the maximum number of participants for an activity is reached before the closing date. Or if the tournament directors decide otherwise.

Registration is only complete when we have received your complete payment.

Payments have to be done online (via Stripe).

The minimum age for participation to any of the tournaments is 18 years. Underage participants can be admitted if the Organisation has received a written permission from the parents or legal guardian of the Participant to take part in the tournament and if the Organisation approves it.

The Participant must fill in all necessary information truthfully. Failure to do so may result in removal or repositioning of the registration. The Organisation can cancel any registration which does not meet the registration conditions or which seems unacceptable.

Possible modifications to existing registrations should be made via the official website with your registration ID before the registration closing date. They are only official when confirmed. Unclear requests will be handled with a lower priority.

When the maximum number of participants for an activity is reached, its registration will be closed. You can contact the tournament director to check if additional places come available. Additional participants will be allocated based on the registration date.

The Organisation does not take any responsibility in case the Participant books overlapping activities

A request for cancellation must be notified by email to your tournament director (see your sport specific page) and is only official after confirmation of receipt.

A refund will be given in the event of cancellation prior to 1 September 2026. A refund after 1 September 2026 cannot be guaranteed, unless the Participant is able to find a person to replace them. The replacement is only official when confirmed.

The cancellation fee for any full or partial cancellation is equal to the transaction costs incurred by the Organisation. In other words, the transaction cost will not be reimbursed to The Participant.
