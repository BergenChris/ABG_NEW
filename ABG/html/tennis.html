Tennis Tournament

Friday – Sunday, November 6–8, 2026

-

Get ready for exciting matches, great rallies, and a fantastic weekend of tennis competition!

Go to Registration

Timing & Schedule

  • Start of matches: 8:00 AM
  • Last matches: Around 10:00 PM
  • Finals & Ceremony: Final matches on Sunday around 3:00 PM, followed by the prize ceremony around 5:00 PM.

Location & Surfaces

Venue: Sporting Club Hove

Address: Elzenstraat 33, 2540 Hove

Surfaces: The tournament will be held on two different indoor surfaces: carpet and hardcourt.

Shoe Policy: For both surfaces, players must wear proper tennis shoes with a smooth, non-marking sole suitable for indoor courts.

Categories & Format

  • Singles (Men & Women)
  • Doubles (Men, Women & Mixed)
  • Consolation rounds available for singles players.

Catering & Extras

  • The venue features a fully equipped club bar and restaurant where you can relax between matches, enjoy a meal, or grab drinks with fellow competitors.
