ABG Closing Party

Celebrate, dance, and connect at the official Antwerp Brilliant Games party!

After a thrilling weekend full of competitive sports, teamwork, and sweat, it is time to let loose and celebrate together. The official closing party of the Antwerp Brilliant Games takes place on Saturday, November 7, 2026.

Location: Woodpop

We are thrilled to host our legendary closing party at Woodpop, one of Antwerp's most vibrant and popular queer-friendly party spots located right in the heart of the city.

Expect fantastic tunes, great drinks, a wonderful inclusive atmosphere, and an unforgettable night surrounded by athletes, friends, and supporters from all over.

Practical Details

  • Date: Saturday, November 7, 2026
  • Time: Starting from 10:00 PM onwards
  • Access: Included for tournament participants or available via ticket info at the venue.
